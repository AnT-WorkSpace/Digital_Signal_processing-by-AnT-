## 🎯 Objectives
* Represent and manipulate signals in the time and frequency domain.
* Describe and analyze properties of discrete-time linear systems using discrete-time transforms.
* Design linear discrete-time filters with prescribed properties.
* Evaluate realizations of discrete-time systems based on canonical structures.
* Understand the impact of finite word-length effects.
* Determine the impact of sampling and quantization in analogue-to-digital conversion.
* Apply (Fast) Fourier-Transforms (FFT) to analyze discrete-time signal processing systems.
* Use Python (NumPy, SciPy) to analyze and synthesize discrete-time signals and systems.

---

## 📚 Course Roadmap & Topics

### 1. Discrete-Time Signals and Systems
* Time-domain description, impulse response, convolution.
* Linearity, time-invariance, causality, and stability

### 2. Frequency-Domain Representation
* Frequency response
* Discrete-time Fourier-Transform (DTFT) and properties

### 3. Analysis of LTI-Systems
* The z-Transform and properties
* System transfer function
* Allpass, minimum-phase, and linear-phase systems

### 4. Sampling and Reconstruction
* Sampling of band-limited signals
* Analogue-to-digital conversion and quantisation

### 5. Digital Filter Design
* Windowing techniques and optimization
* Bilinear transform and band-transforms

### 6. Filter Realization
* Filter structures and signal flow graphs
* Transposition methods (canonical, cascade, and parallel architectures)

### 7. Multi-Rate Systems
* Decimation and interpolation
* Oversampling analogue-to-digital conversion

### 8. Discrete Fourier-Transform (DFT)
* Fast algorithms (FFT) using decimation in time and frequency
* Applications of the DFT
